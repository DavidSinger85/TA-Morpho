# The 24 test pictures

Test data of TA-Morpho: 24 pictures of tests of 13 hyalospheniid genera and *Argynnia*, 9 by light
microscopy, 10 by SEM and 5 drawings, each a panel cut from a published figure. Picture ids: P-numbers
are ids of the author's working collection; TB*nnnX* = Todorov & Bankov (2019), page *nnn*, panel *X*.

Every picture except the drawings holds a scale bar, and its length is in the file name (e.g. `_20um`),
so the program calibrates them by itself. Where the panel's bar was outside the published panel, or could
not be read by the program, it was taken from the same figure at the same scale, as said in the Scale
column. Drawings have no scale. Panel letters and fragments of neighbouring panels were left as published.
